Who we are

A lifeline for patients, survivors and the people who love them.

BeeOba Lifeline Initiatives is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting cancer patients, survivors and their families, as well as individuals facing serious health challenges. We exist to make a heavy journey a little lighter.

From the first frightening conversation to the long road of recovery, we help individuals and families navigate difficult health situations with dignity, hope and care — connecting them to information, resources and people who understand.

  • Emotional and peer support for patients, survivors and caregivers
  • Health awareness campaigns and early-detection education
  • Advocacy that puts patients’ voices at the centre of care
  • Practical assistance with transport, nutrition and daily essentials

What we do

Support that meets real needs

Our programs are built around the questions families actually ask — “What now?”, “Who can help?” and “How do we get through this?”

Compassionate support

One-to-one listening, support groups and peer companions who understand the emotional weight of a diagnosis.

Health awareness

Community education on cancer prevention, early detection and healthy living — delivered in schools, markets, workplaces and places of worship.

Advocacy

We speak up for affordable treatment, patient rights and better access to care — and we help families speak up for themselves.

Practical assistance

Care packages, nutrition support, transport to treatment and help with everyday essentials when income is stretched.

Family & caregiver care

Because illness touches everyone at home: counselling, respite and guidance for spouses, children and caregivers.

Navigation & referrals

Help understanding diagnoses, treatment options, paperwork and where to go next — with trusted referrals and follow-up.

How we help

Four simple steps to support

No paperwork maze. No cost. Just reach out and we will walk the rest of the way with you.

1

Reach out

Call, email or send us a message. A family member or friend can contact us on your behalf.

2

Talk with us

A confidential conversation about your situation, your worries and what you need most right now.

3

Build a plan

Together we map emotional, practical and informational support around your treatment and family life.

4

Walk together

Ongoing check-ins, peer groups, advocacy and assistance for as long as the journey lasts.
